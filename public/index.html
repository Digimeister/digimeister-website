▸ ABOUT DIGIMEISTER

Digimeister is a game studio in Fukuoka, Japan, founded in 2022. We make and port games for PC, console and mobile, with other studios and on our own.

▸ WHAT WE DO

Co-dev and programming: porting, netcode and live systems. Since 2024 our focus has been online games, the multiplayer kind that only work because other players are in them.
